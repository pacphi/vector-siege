# Vector Siege Player Guide

## Your mission

Survive as many waves as possible. Hostile units appear around the arena and become more numerous as your wave count rises. Use the radar and the angular obstacles to avoid being surrounded.

You begin with three tanks. A collision or enemy shot costs one tank. After a hit you briefly flash and cannot be damaged, giving you time to reposition. Your best score is saved in this browser.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drive forward / reverse | `W` / `S` or `↑` / `↓` | — |
| Turn | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | Move left / right while pointer is captured |
| Fire | `Space` | Primary click |
| Pause / resume | `P` or `Esc` | — |
| Mute / unmute | `M` | Sound button |
| Full screen | `F` | Fullscreen button |

Select **Start Mission** to begin. Clicking the battlefield captures the pointer for mouse steering; press `Esc` to release it. Keyboard-only play remains fully supported.

## Reading the cockpit

- The center reticle shows where your cannon fires.
- Radar blips show the relative direction and distance of nearby contacts.
- Red tanks are steady attackers, amber scouts are faster, and magenta drones are tougher.
- Gold saucers do not attack. They are optional high-value targets.
- The outer arena wall is a boundary; scenery provides visual cover but cannot be destroyed.

## Scoring

| Target | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Tank | 100 |
| Fast scout | 150 |
| Homing drone | 250 |
| Bonus saucer | 500 |

Tip: keep moving after firing. Scouts close distance quickly, while drones take two hits.

## Comfort and troubleshooting

Pause whenever you need a break. The game automatically pauses if its browser tab becomes hidden. Sound is synthesized in the browser and starts only after you interact with the game. If controls do not respond, click the battlefield once and make sure the game is not paused.
